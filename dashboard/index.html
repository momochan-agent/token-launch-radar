Token Launch Radar

Protocols that record no token, ranked by what a token would be worth if their record stayed as it is. Implied value is this board’s own claim — not a prediction that a token will be issued, and never a promise of an airdrop.

How the ranking works (model radar-1)

Implied value = TVL × category peer multiple × growth × maturity × chains.

  • Peer multiple: for each category, the median market-cap / TVL of protocols whose token already trades in this snapshot (global median when a category has fewer than 3 traded peers). Traded protocols are the comparables — the board itself lists pre-token protocols only.
  • Growth: 7-day TVL change, +35% at +100% a week, −10.5% at −30%.
  • Maturity: days listed with no token recorded, 0.85 at listing rising to 1.00 after ~3 years.
  • Chains: +3% per extra chain, capped at +12%.

DefiLlama’s record is not proof: a protocol “records no token”, never “has none”. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are excluded from the watchlist, as are bridges, liquid-staking products and exchange products. Funding, revenue, launch dates and airdrops are not in the snapshot and are never invented here.

Traded peers — the comparables (market values, not ranked)

Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart from the board and shown here at the market’s own value. Their median mcap/TVL by category is the multiple the board uses.
